Scenario Guide · v1.2

Transfer Item Between Warehouses

move a quantity of one item from a source warehouse to a destination warehouse, both in the same logistic company, using a manually created warehousing order — and verify that on-hand at the source dropped and on-hand at the destination rose by the same quantity.

Completion BoundarySource warehouse on-hand down and destination warehouse on-hand up by the transferred quantity
