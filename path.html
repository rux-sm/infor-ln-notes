Recommended Order

Learning Path

The order follows data dependencies — what must exist before what — rather than menus or modules. Each stage says why it comes where it does; read a guide first, then run it against a training environment. Statuses are shown openly: Draft means exactly that.

Stage 1Build the master dataDraftv3.6203 Steps

Create Basic Test Items and Defaults

Start here: every other procedure consumes master data. This guide builds a small, self-contained training family — items, defaults, bill of material, routing, costing, and component stock — and its final phase hands a qualified case to the production guide.

Completion BoundaryThree base test items with verified Purchase and Job Shop defaults, an approved bill of material and routing, confirmed costing, and component stock — a training case ready for a production order
